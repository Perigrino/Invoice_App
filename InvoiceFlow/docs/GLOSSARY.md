# GLOSSARY

| Term | Means | Not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invoice | A request for payment, with line items, dates, and a balance due | bill, document, record |
| Estimate | A quoted job that can become an Invoice | quote, proposal |
| Recurring Profile | A generator template that creates Invoices on a schedule | recurring invoice, copy |
| Client | The person or company you bill | customer, contact |
| Company | Your sender profile: name, address, tax ids | my account, business |
| Payment | A money-in record against one Invoice: amount, date, method, note | transaction, receipt |
| Line Item | One row on an Invoice: name, description, qty, rate, taxes | item, product |
| Amount Due | Total shown on the invoice PDF to the client | total |
| Balance Due | Total minus recorded payments (never negative) | remaining, owed |
| Discount | Reduction applied before tax | credit, deposit |
| Deposit | Money requested before or during delivery | down payment |
| Credit | Reduction taken from funds already held | discount |
| Tax | Sales tax or VAT/GST line, up to 2 per Line Item | GST, VAT (that's the label naming) |
| Terms | Payment rule that derives the due date (e.g. Net 14) | payment terms |
| Due Date | When money is owed | expiry |
| Status | Draft, Sent, Outstanding, Overdue, Partially Paid, Paid | state, phase |
| Archive | Hide a finished Invoice without deleting; excluded from reports | delete, trash |
| New Invoice | Create an invoice (⌘N) | add invoice |
| Add Payment… | Open the payment sheet | pay, mark paid |
| Record Payment | Confirm button in the payment sheet | save, confirm |
| Export CSV… | Write invoices/clients/payments to CSV | backup |
| Restore from Backup… | Roll the store back to a snapshot | import, undo |

Style: Title Case for buttons/menus/titles; sentence case for labels and messages. Errors state what happened and the fix, no blame ("Enter an amount.").
